I'm Ara Aranya — the creator behind Nova Badzlina Global.

I make content that feels like a recommendation from a friend rather than an advertisement. Whether it's a twenty-second hook for TikTok, a thirty-day account build, a film review or a voice-over session, the job is always the same: make people stop, watch, and trust.

Everything in this catalogue is produced from my own studio — I write, film, perform, edit and deliver each piece myself. That means no coordination between three vendors, no version drift, and one point of contact from brief to final file.

For brands who need me physically present, I attend one and two-day events across Peninsular Malaysia, from my base in Kota Bharu, Kelantan. Everywhere else in the world, the work is delivered remotely — and nothing is lost, because the deliverables are identical.

NBInvestment11

Working together

From first message to final file

  1. You send the brief. Brand, deliverables, deadline, and where the content will run. WhatsApp, email or the booking form on my website — whichever is easiest.
  2. I send a quote. Price, deliverables, timeline and usage rights, in writing. Change anything you like at this stage — revisions to the brief are free.
  3. We both sign. A short written agreement covering the work, the payment schedule, revisions, usage rights and what happens if either of us has to cancel.
  4. You pay the deposit. 10%–50%, your choice. The booking is confirmed and your dates are held.
  5. I produce the work and send you a watermarked draft — full length, full quality — so you can review it properly.
  6. You give feedback within three working days, consolidated into one message where possible.
  7. You settle the balance and I release the clean, watermark-free final files the same day.
  8. Delivery by WeTransfer, Blip or Google Drive — you choose which.

What I need from you

  • The product at least 5 days before
  • Brand guidelines, must-say lines, no-go list
  • Feedback within 3 working days

What you can expect from me

  • A reply within 24 hours on working days
  • A watermarked draft before any balance is due
  • Full refund if I ever have to cancel
