Before you start

Registering with SSM

Everything you will be asked for, with the wording already written. Keep this open in one window and EzBiz in the other, and copy across.

Check the live figures before you pay. Fees, forms and the MSIC code list are set by SSM and do change. Everything here is a preparation aid so you are not writing your business description in a panic at 1am — the numbers on ezbiz.ssm.com.my are the ones that count. If anything in the form contradicts this sheet, the form is right.

What you are registering as

A sole proprietorship (Perniagaan Milik Tunggal) — one owner, registered under the Registration of Businesses Act. It is the right shape for you now: cheapest, fastest, no company secretary, no audited accounts. You can convert to an Sdn Bhd later if you ever need to take on a partner or limit liability.

Two ways to register the name

OptionWhat it meansNotes
Personal name
Nama sendiri		 The business trades under your own name exactly as it appears on your IC — Nur Badzlina Razi binti Mohamed Gazali. Cheaper, and no name approval needed. But it is not your brand.
Trade name
Nama tred		 The business trades as NOVA BADZLINA GLOBAL. Costs more per year and needs name approval first. This is the one you want — it matches your website, invoices, contracts and bank account.

Why the trade name matters for you specifically. Every document you already have — the booking agreement, invoices, receipts, rate card — says NOVA BADZLINA GLOBAL. A brand paying you wants the name on the invoice to match the name on the bank account. Registering the personal name only would mean redoing all of it.

Have these ready before you open the form

  1. Your MyKad — number and full name exactly as printed.
  2. An EzBiz account — register at ezbiz.ssm.com.my first. First-time users must verify their identity; check the current method on the site, as it has changed before (counter verification has been required in the past).
  3. Your business address in Kota Bharu, Kelantan, with postcode.
  4. A start date for the business — see page 2.
  5. Online banking or a card to pay the fee.
  6. Two or three backup names in case the first is rejected — see page 2.

What to type

The name

Business name — type exactly thisNOVA BADZLINA GLOBAL

Have backups ready. SSM restricts certain words in business names, and approval is not guaranteed. If GLOBAL is queried, do not panic and do not accept a name you dislike just to get through — you would be stuck with it on every invoice. Try, in this order:

  1. NOVA BADZLINA GLOBAL
  2. NOVA BADZLINA STUDIO
  3. NOVA BADZLINA CREATIVE
  4. ARA ARANYA STUDIO

Anything except the first would mean updating your website and documents — tell me and I will sweep every file in one pass.

The fiddly part

MSIC codes

EzBiz asks you to pick MSIC codes — standard classification numbers for what your business does. You normally select more than one. This is the field people get wrong, and a wrong code can cause questions later when you open a bank account.

Do not copy code numbers from anywhere, including this page. MSIC codes are revised, and quoting a stale number is worse than searching properly. Use the search box inside EzBiz and pick from what it actually returns.

Search these words in the MSIC picker

Pick the advertising one as your primary if the form asks for a main activity. It is the broadest umbrella for what you actually sell, and it is the one a bank will recognise immediately as a real trade.

After it is approved

  1. Download the certificate and the Business Info printout. Keep both in the same folder as your contracts.
  2. Open a business bank account in the business name. Maybank will want the certificate, the printout and your IC. This is the real prize — a client paying NOVA BADZLINA GLOBAL rather than a personal account looks materially more serious.
  3. Send me the registration number and I will add it to your invoices, receipts, booking agreement, rate card and website footer. Brands and agencies often ask for it before they can raise a purchase order.
  4. Diarise the renewal before it expires. A lapsed registration is a re-registration.

One thing to be aware of. A sole proprietorship is not a separate legal person — the business and you are the same in law, so business debts are personal debts. At your scale that is normally fine, and it is what almost every creator starts with. It is only worth revisiting if you ever take on staff, sign a large liability, or bring in a partner.
