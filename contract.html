9 If a booking is cancelled

  • Party B cancels before work begins — the deposit is retained. It covers the slot held and other work turned away.
  • Party B cancels after work has begun — the full agreed price is due. Party B receives everything completed to that point.
  • Party A cancels — if illness, family emergency or any cause on Party A's side prevents delivery, Party B receives a full refund including the deposit, or a rescheduled date at no extra cost. Party B chooses which.
  • Rescheduling — free once if more than 7 days before the date. Within 7 days, a rescheduled in-person booking is treated as last-minute.

Once a booking is confirmed and the deposit is paid, it is non-refundable except in the case described above where Party A cancels.

For monthly projects, posting and production pause until the outstanding month is settled.
