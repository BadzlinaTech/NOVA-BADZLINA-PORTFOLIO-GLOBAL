TO PUT IT ON THE INTERNET
-------------------------
  The site itself is small. The videos are not — they are about 2 GB, and
  free hosting rejects files that size. So there are two steps:

  STEP 1 — Upload the videos to YouTube
    Go to studio.youtube.com, upload everything in media/video/, and set
    each one to UNLISTED (not Private — private blocks embedding).
    Use the filenames as the titles.
    Send the links to Claude and the players get swapped over.

  STEP 2 — Deploy
    Go to  app.netlify.com/drop
    Drag this whole folder onto the page.
    You get a live web address in about 30 seconds. No account needed.

    For your own domain (novabadzlinaglobal.com, around RM 55/year),
    buy it at Namecheap or Cloudflare, then point it in Netlify's
    Domain settings.


ONE THING TO FIX AFTER YOU HAVE A DOMAIN
----------------------------------------
  Every page has  <meta property="og:image" content="media/photo/profile.jpg">
  That path only works locally. Once you have a real address it needs to be
  the full URL, or WhatsApp and Instagram will not show your photo when
  someone shares your link. Tell Claude the domain and it gets fixed on all
  24 pages in one pass.
