The voice rule

Half to record, half to release

Voice work splits down the middle. The deposit gets me into the booth; the balance releases the clean master. In between you get a voice-tagged draft — full performance, full length, with a spoken tag over it — so you can approve the read before you pay the rest.

  2. Tagged draft — you hear the full read with a voice tag over it and request your pickups.
  3. 50% balance — settled once you are happy with the read.
  4. Clean master — tag removed, delivered as broadcast-ready audio the same day.

Product photography · Photo editing · Commercial talent · Short acting · Canvas makeup · Travel · Wedding

The day rate rule

We agree the rate first. I invoice when the day is done.

Anything that takes a whole day of my time is billed by the day rather than by the piece. The rate, the number of days and what is included are all agreed in writing before we start — then I invoice once the day or days are complete, and the final edited files follow the invoice.
